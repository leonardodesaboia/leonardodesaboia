# Leonardo de Saboia

**Full Stack Developer · Software Engineer · Fortaleza, Brazil**

I build web, mobile and desktop products from idea to production, working across software architecture, applied AI, integrations and data-driven systems. My experience includes technical leadership and project management in Brazilian and international projects.

I'm in the **8th semester of Computer Science at UNIFOR**, with graduation expected in **June 2027**. Most recently, I worked as a Full Stack Developer at **Moldsoft** (July–September 2026). Previously, I was an Innovation & Development Intern at **Vortex Lab / UNIFOR** (February 2025–July 2026) and Founder & CTO of **AllSet** (April 2025–May 2026).
